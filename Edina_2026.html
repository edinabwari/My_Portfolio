The context behind my work

 

I work across the whole data lifecycle: I clean and check the data, build models and reports from it, and design the apps and dashboards that put it in front of the people who need it. My background is in information technology, and I have built websites, mobile apps, and analytics projects.

My projects cover sustainability, healthcare, education, mental health, and agriculture. Each one started with a real problem, from unreliable waste data to farmers who can't diagnose crop disease, and each ended with something people could use.
