The context behind my work

 

My work starts with understanding the problem behind the data. Where does the information come from? How reliable is it? What does it tell us, and how can it be used to make better decisions? With a background in Information Technology, I work across data analysis, research and digital solutions. I am interested in the connection between collecting reliable information, understanding what it reveals and building tools that make it useful.

My projects respond to real-world challenges that people and organisations face. Driven by my interest in data and technology, I explore how these tools can be used to understand those challenges and develop practical solutions. I believe the real value of data and technology is in their ability to help us understand problems, make informed decisions and find better ways of doing things.

The projects presented here demonstrate this approach through practical work, interactive demos and source code. Where real records were involved, data has been anonymised or synthesised to protect privacy.

I am open to opportunities in research, data analytics, MEAL and digital solutions where I can contribute to better information, stronger systems and informed decisions.
